A romhack is what happens when someone opens up a finished game, finds the bytes that make the ducks and the cops and the golfers, and decides they should be something else. Done well, a romhack gives an old game a second life. This is not about the done well ones. Today we're looking at the other kind: the crude, clunky, quite possibly unrequested kind, built on launch games every Nintendo fan knows by heart. That's what makes them so strange. It's like finding your childhood dog in a tiny hat.

I've given each hack two scores: how **weird** it is, and whether it's **worth a play**. And because you shouldn't have to take my word for it, every hack comes with the original game alongside it. Drag the slider to see what changed.

**What it does:** A small modification that changes the season to autumn, makes the dog do the moon walk, and includes an 8-bit rendition of Michael Jackson's moon walk.

**Review:** I'll admit I'm impressed that the dog does the moon walk, even if it's really just his animation sprite in reverse. The change to autumn is really comfy, and I'd love to see a winter version as well. It also makes me think we should do a romhack that adds rain to further complicate and obscure the ducks. ### Soccer: World Cup 2002

<div class="meters"><div class="mtr w" role="img" aria-label="Weird: 5 of 5"><b>Weird</b><i></i><i></i><i></i><i></i><i></i></div><div class="mtr p" role="img" aria-label="Worth a play: 2 of 5"><b>Worth a play</b><i></i><i></i><i class="off"></i><i class="off"></i><i class="off"></i></div></div>

<div class="cmp" role="group" aria-label="Soccer original (left) compared with the World Cup 2002 hack (right)" style="--o:url({BASE}/media/romhack-original-soccer.png);--h:url({BASE}/media/463cf574-3052screenshot1.png)"></div>

**What it does:** This modification replaces Japan with China, adds a new logo to represent the 2002 World Cup, and, of course, turns all the players into rabbits.

**Review:** Did you do a double take there? Yeah, I did too. For starters, the 2002 World Cup was co-hosted by Japan and South Korea, so swapping Japan out for China (who did play in the cup) seems really weird. Especially when you consider that Japan made it to the round of 16 and China did not. The redone title screen is pretty boring next to the bold, light-up action of NES Soccer, but that's not a huge deal. Romhacks lead to change. What I can't understand is the rabbits. It's pretty good sprite work, I might add. After some extensive googling, all I could figure out is that Duracell was a sponsor of the 2002 World Cup and produced plush bunnies with the World Cup logo on them. That doesn't really explain anything, because the actual mascots of the 2002 World Cup were three abominations known as Ato, Nik and Kaz.

[Get the hack on romhacking.net](http://www.romhacking.net/hacks/3052/)

### Kung-Fu: Sylvia Saves Thomas

<div class="meters"><div class="mtr w" role="img" aria-label="Weird: 2 of 5"><b>Weird</b><i></i><i></i><i class="off"></i><i class="off"></i><i class="off"></i></div><div class="mtr p" role="img" aria-label="Worth a play: 3 of 5"><b>Worth a play</b><i></i><i></i><i></i><i class="off"></i><i class="off"></i></div></div>

<div class="cmp" role="group" aria-label="Kung-Fu original (left) compared with the Sylvia Saves Thomas hack (right)" style="--o:url({BASE}/media/romhack-original-kung-fu.png);--h:url({BASE}/media/e7376db0-Kung-Fu-010.png)"></div>

**What it does:** The roles of Thomas and Sylvia are reversed. Sylvia must now save her boyfriend, Thomas, from the evil Mr. X.

**Review:** So this is exactly Kung-Fu, but now you play as Sylvia. That might sound like a pretty boring romhack, but there's some lore behind this one. Back before the internet, there was a rumor that if you beat Kung-Fu fifty times, you'd get to play as Sylvia in the next round. That is not only cruel, it's likely impossible. The rumor was so widespread that a version of it ended up in an official [GameFAQs walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/587396-kung-fu/faqs/30551), with the twist that if you beat the game 50 times you actually fight Sylvia instead of Mr. X. Kelly R. Flewin debunked it on [Twin Galaxies](https://www.twingalaxies.com/archive/index.php/t-112110.html) by beating the game 53 times and never fighting Sylvia. It's nice to have a hack where we play as Sylvia, but I'd love to see the other version come true, just for Mr. Flewin's sake.

[Get the hack on romhacking.net](http://www.romhacking.net/hacks/1771/)

I'm giving this one the thumbs up, if only for the polished skeleton sprites.

**What it does:** The FBI has decided to change its training facility to prepare for the upcoming invasion of the Mushroom Kingdom. The gangsters are now Bowser and Donkey Kong.

**Review:** Honestly, this one is pretty cool, and the art deserves major props. The Mario who replaces the cop looks as much like the cop as he does Mario. It's really strange to see Bowser and Donkey Kong holding guns, if only because Super Mario Bros. and Donkey Kong would have been incredibly tough if those two had weaponized.

### Golf: Starring KISS

<div class="meters"><div class="mtr w" role="img" aria-label="Weird: 5 of 5"><b>Weird</b><i></i><i></i><i></i><i></i><i></i></div><div class="mtr p" role="img" aria-label="Worth a play: 3 of 5"><b>Worth a play</b><i></i><i></i><i></i><i class="off"></i><i class="off"></i></div></div>

**What it does:** Tee up for 18 holes, but this time you play as the members of KISS.

**Review:** Of course this one had to be saved for last. What a bizarre creation. Beautiful, accurate KISS sprite work meets the classic 18 holes of NES Golf. If you happen to be a huge fan of Golf specifically on the NES and also a huge fan of KISS, today is a big day for you. The question I'll leave you with is: why? Are KISS known for being into golf? I know Alice Cooper is actually a big golfer, and it probably would have made more sense to make Golf: Welcome to My Nightmare. But I think it's really nice that this exists.
