# 379 - Ultimate Basketball

Ultimate Basketball is about as close as we have come to a box back that is just the title again. It says "It's the ultimate," then "This is it." We wondered if it would be the definitive basketball game or an extreme version with new rules, like Ultimate Frisbee. It is just basketball, with made-up teams. Mike, Sean and Joe were on this one.

### What works

- **Shooting.** Press the button once to jump and again to release, and the closer you are to the top of the jump, the better the shot. It feels more modern than the other basketball games we have played, and it was the one part of the game we had no notes on.
- **The dunk cutscenes.** They are smoother than the slideshow dunk in Double Dribble, and you can take part in a dunk or a block by hitting a small glowing section of a meter. The manual's pitch for the three-pointer and free throw cutscenes is to sit back and enjoy the show.
- **The team names.** The Detroit Unions have two fists on their logo and were the team we played as. The Houston Comets at least make sense, and the New York Powers sent us on an inflatable-rat tangent.
- **A bright game.** It is colorful, and the cutscenes break up the monotony.

### What doesn't

- **The computer cheats.** There is no steal button and you can't pick off a pass, but the computer picks off our inbound throws and steals the ball constantly. On one inbound pass Mike rewound with an emulator several times and still could not find a man open before the ball was gone.
- **Violations everywhere.** We got called for charging just for walking into somebody. Foul out enough players and you can be left with three on the court.
- **Dunks eat inputs.** Sometimes the meter ignores your button press and you don't get the dunk.
- **A tournament with nothing in it.** It is a string of exhibitions with the same reward. With no real teams, you can't have a Mets-Yankees dream matchup.
- **The team select screen.** Left and right move you to the next team on a list, not the team in that direction, and Joe said it is worse than the Sesame Street one.

### Beyond the game

Acom made it, along with Omegon and Ashton X, and also an Ultimate Football that became Football Fury on the Super Nintendo. We could not confirm if this is the last basketball game on the NES.

### The bottom line

Skip it. All three of us voted no on the Essential Games List. Mike didn't enjoy playing the computer and wouldn't seek it out with a friend either. Sean said the ingredients are right and the cooking time is off. Joe felt like it was a chore, and a step back from what he remembers of the earlier basketball games.
