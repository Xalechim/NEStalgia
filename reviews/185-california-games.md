# 185 - California Games

Rare made this for Milton Bradley, a collection of sports minigames themed around California. Mike and Sean played without Joe. We liked a couple of events, got frustrated by the rest, and called it not essential.

### What works

- **Footbag.** The standout. The bag has some physics, so it drifts left or right even on a clean hit. The timing is forgiving, with something like a hitbox on your foot, and there are lots of tricks to discover. Your head turns out to be the main tool.
- **The flying disc.** Easy to understand, with golf-style meters. There is always a better way to approach it than what you did. It is oversimplified, but has more depth than the screen suggests. You get one attempt, and we wanted a best of three.
- **Surfing's judges.** Being scored out of 10 gives you a frame of reference, since 50,000 points means nothing. That sent us off on ranking arcade games with letter grades. The shark that appears when you wipe out is a fun gag once, then it kills you every time.

### What doesn't

- **The timing.** Skateboarding is the worst. Holding up and down to build speed on the half pipe is tactile, but almost any trick means falling and eating dirt. We could only land kick turns and hand stalls. We think this series never got its timing mechanics right, and the events with more leeway were the ones we liked.
- **Roller skating.** An obstacle course where she can't hop a crack in the sidewalk. We wanted to play the best roller skater ever, not a novice tripping over herself.
- **BMX.** Another obstacle course, with tires strewn around it. Mike, exhausted by this point, skipped it. After Sean described it, it sounded stressful.

### Worth knowing

- **Thin California vibe.** The title card tried to be a license plate but missed the font. It feels like the pop culture idea of California.
- **No hub.** We wished it had Skate or Die's boardwalk to walk around.
- **It sold well.** More than 300,000 copies in nine months, outselling every other game in the series. California Games II sold less.
- **1001 Video Games You Must Play Before You Die.** It is in there, and we found the whole idea of that book ridiculous.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List. It is a sampler, the birth of the mini game, and footbag and the disc are the picks. Most of the rest has been improved on since, so you can ignore this one.
