# 174 - Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde

Advance Communication Company made this for Bandai in 1989. The goal is a race to the church to marry Miss Millicent, and if your stress meter fills, Jekyll turns into Mr. Hyde and you land in a demon world. We found it absurd and bad in fun ways.

### Jekyll's London

- **Everyone is out to get you.** Guys walk in front of you, drop a bomb and run off. Kids fire slingshots, birds fall on your head, and the black cats are harmless until they start bugging out.
- **You can only walk.** You can't run from a bomb, and we couldn't find a use for the cane. Crouching in front of a bee or fly makes it vanish with a little boop, the only interaction we found.
- **Designed to stress you out.** As we put it: "the problem with a game designed to stress you out is that it stresses you out. And that's not fun."

### Hyde's demon world

Hyde's world is mirrored, so you walk right to left, and it auto-scrolls. Now there are real enemies, and you have the psycho wave, which is on the front of the box. It dips below the ground and loops back to hit things from behind, and you can't control it.

Reach the spot you got to as Jekyll and you die. Hyde is more fun, but only because Jekyll is so awful. We never fully understood the bribery system, where you pay people to stop being jerks with money you find as Hyde.

### Two endings

Reach the church as Jekyll and that is the bad ending: no cutscene, just the end, and you reset your NES. To see the wedding, you need your stress meter full near the church so you can become Hyde on the final level, where the lightning bolt that kills you is gone. Then you beat a floating demon head that vanishes and reappears and takes many psycho waves.

### Worth knowing

- **The reputation.** The Angry Video Game Nerd made a 48-second video of how fast you die, and Wikipedia lists it among the worst games of all time. Joe would score it down with the ones and zeros out of ten but says plenty on the NES are worse.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List. Joe doesn't think it would be a good game even if it were executed properly. Sean called it bad in fun ways, but still bad. Mike was sold on 40 more minutes by the first bomb drop but won't say you should play it, and says anyone can find out what it is about in 10 minutes, so it goes on our fastest games to play list.
