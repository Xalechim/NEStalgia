# 247 - Short Order / Eggsplode!

TOSE made this for Nintendo in 1989, and as usual took none of the credit. It is two Power Pad games on one cartridge, the last Power Pad games we have to play. Short Order is Simon meets Overcooked. Eggsplode! is whack-a-mole with bombs and hens. We liked the concepts more than the games.

### Short Order

- **Simon in a burger shop.** Animals order burgers and you build them by jumping on the pad: 1 and 2 for a beef patty, 3 and 4 for cheese. Each time you get it right, the same customer adds one more ingredient and you rebuild the whole stack. Every ingredient has its own tone, so it covers visual and auditory learners.
- **Nothing here needs your feet.** It is pure memorization, with no speed, coordination or dexterity. Our better ideas: a dance club burger shop where you hop to a beat, or a kitchen with the ingredients spread around, hopping like a rabbit.
- **Height is the whole difficulty.** Level one is a mouse and a 10-story burger, and it goes up to 99. The menu looks like many modes, but nothing really changes between them.

### Eggsplode!

- **Foxes, hens and bombs.** Foxes run across the screen laying bombs under hens, and you hop over to stomp them out. Other hens lay eggs, and stepping on an egg is the fail state. Bombs go off faster depending on color.
- **Whack-a-mole.** The fun of whack-a-mole is the tactile whacking, and stomping is the next best thing to a mallet, so this one makes more sense on the pad. As for what your feet are doing to the bombs, we settled on scissor feet.
- **Rhythm.** Later rounds get fast, and if you fall out of rhythm you can go ten seconds hitting nothing.

### Worth knowing

- **How Joe played.** He could not get a Power Pad working in an emulator, so he folded a gridded quilted blanket into three rows of four and jumped along with a YouTube video, on an honor system. He also whistled each ingredient's tone to remember the order.
- **The menu screen.** The Eggsplode fox bombs the Short Order title away, and the pig and mouse hop onto the Eggsplode one and give the camera a sly look. Joe watched it for a good while.
- **The pad.** There is no controller-only option, and a rhythm game never showed up.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List. It is Simon and whack-a-mole, and there is not enough there to carry either game. Still, we enjoyed the cartoony, weird-peripheral charm, and among Power Pad games it ranks pretty high, ahead of the running simulators.
