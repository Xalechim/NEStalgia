# 402 - Yo! Noid

Yo! Noid is a Capcom-published side-scrolling platformer starring the Domino's mascot, and then nobody put Domino's in it. Mike and Sean were on this one, and we spent more of the episode on the Noid than on the cartridge.

### The Noid himself

- **Not a Domino's fan.** The Noid is a saboteur trying to ruin your delivery before the 30-minute mark, so unlike the Hamburglar he doesn't love the product. In the game he hates pizza, and Domino's barely appears.
- **A very strange true story.** In 1989 a Georgia man named Kenneth Lamar Noid held two Domino's employees hostage. In the version we read, he put the gun down after they made him pizza. The mascot wasn't retired for about five more years, so the theory that this ended him doesn't hold much water.
- **Later cameos.** A web game, Noid's Super Pizza Shootout, in 2011 for the chain's 25th birthday, and a Domino's pinball machine in 2016.

### What works

- **Levels with a trick.** The first pier level has a tide that rises and washes you away for an instant death, and the first death there is confusing. There's also a skateboard stage and an ice stage with real slipping. None of it changes the game much, but it isn't one mechanic repeated.
- **Variety.** Each stage has its own tile set and enemies, there's a colorful world map, and a good number of stages. It never gives you five water levels in a row.
- **A boomerang weapon.** The yo-yo comes back to you, which is always a good weapon, and the pace stays steady.

### What doesn't

- **The pizza contest.** Every level ends with a card game against a colored Noid: lay down pizza counts and win points by beating the opponent's number. We found it mostly waiting around to out-math the opponent. Lose, and you replay the whole level, not the contest.
- **Edge-of-platform jumps.** For a mascot game, some jumps need you on the lip of a platform. A rabbit-eared guy should jump better. A few enemies take several hits, and one on the piers takes about 20.
- **A yo-yo with no string.** When he idles you see the string, but once thrown it looks like a magic baseball.
- **Identity crisis.** On the box he rides a pizza crusher as a pogo stick, and we kept asking who wanted to play as the Noid.

### Beyond the game

Capcom gave the Japanese game Masked Ninja Hanamaru a visual overhaul. The original ninja throws a bird that comes back like the yo-yo, and its card game has no pizza.

### The bottom line

Skip it. We both voted it off the Essential Games List. It's a perfectly fine platformer and not offensively bad, but the game itself is almost wholly unremarkable. It had more value as a cultural object than as something to play.
