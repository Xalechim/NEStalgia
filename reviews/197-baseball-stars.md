# 197 - Baseball Stars

SNK made this baseball game, and it sets itself apart by letting you run the whole operation: player, manager, owner. It has no licenses, but it has create-a-team, create-a-player and create-a-league. We liked it a lot, and it made the Essential Games List on a split vote.

### What works

- **Customization.** You can use the preset teams or build your own league. You can hire and fire, look at rookies, veterans and superstars, and train players with the money you win. Rookies start weak but have the highest ceiling, while veterans give you stats right away but top out sooner.
- **Control in the batter's box.** You can move up, down, left and right, and where you hit the ball matches where you swung. Pitching has overhand and sidearm, and what you do with the D-pad matters.
- **Seeing the ball.** The shadow and the ball's size made it the first baseball game where we could tell where the ball was.
- **Small touches.** Foul balls get marked on the field. The Lovely Ladies are an all-women's team. You can also build female players, so a mixed team is possible.
- **Conservative baserunning.** With so few superstars, you think about your runner's stats before you send him. If a team gets ahead by 10 runs, the game gets called.

### What doesn't

- **Bare presentation.** The field is one solid green with brown dirt, and the crowd of floating heads is odd.
- **Few logos.** There are only nine or ten, mostly letters.
- **Computer robbery.** The computer can climb the wall and rob your home run.
- **The payoff is slow.** Building a formidable team takes a lot of time. It has a battery backup, so it suits playing now and then.

### Worth knowing

The setup menus ate a lot of our time, and for a menu diver that is where the game shines. You can set up a league with as many as six teams and 25 games, or just three teams at one game each. In a rundown the fielders are fast enough that the runner rarely escapes.

### The bottom line

Play it. Sean voted Essential first, for the customization and the little extras, and Joe joined him, saying the game has done pretty much everything right. Mike left it off. He still thinks RBI Baseball is the better game, and he doesn't think either one is something you must go back to. With two yes votes, Baseball Stars went onto the Essential Games List. If you let this one slip past you, go back and revisit it.
