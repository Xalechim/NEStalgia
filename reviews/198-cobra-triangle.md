# 198 - Cobra Triangle

Rare made this for Nintendo in 1989. It is a cannon-equipped speedboat game, and it plays like RC Pro-Am on water. It has 25 stages with changing objectives. We liked it a lot, apart from a few stages we hated.

### What works

- **The controls.** Joe called it one-to-one and never laggy, one of the few games that feels beyond the NES. You get air off ramps and skip off waves. The controls are relative to the boat, so turning takes getting used to, but it was not a huge flaw.
- **Variety.** Stages have us winning races, saving swimmers, defusing bombs and fighting bosses. The first level is a race, so it misleads you: racing isn't really any of the objectives after that.
- **Gradius-style power-ups.** Collect numbered pods, pick a power-up or keep collecting for a better one. It beats Gradius in one way: you keep your power-ups when you die. Once we started paying attention to them, the game got a lot more fun.
- **Capturing the mines.** You drag the mines back to your base while boats chase you and try to steal them. The AI can't handle zigzagging and runs into islands, which is funny, but this was a highlight. Later stages add a second boat and whirlpools.

### What doesn't

- **The waterfall stages.** You are zoomed in, cannot see the next ramp, and there is only one way forward. Land in a whirlpool and the waves speed you up for the next jump. We called these the worst. The log and iceberg courses are hard, but felt fairer.
- **Scarce pods.** They appear in the first two levels, and then you go a long stretch with none. Miss the pod level and starting over beats carrying on underpowered.
- **Forgettable bosses.** The Loch Ness Monster, a crab, a shark and a squid. There is little room to move, and a boat can't dodge sideways. Joe liked them as a change from shmup bosses.

### Worth knowing

The speedometer at the bottom looks so much like a compass that we took it for one.

Diddy Kong Racing is the through line we saw, not a sequel: boss fights, boat levels, and protecting and capturing eggs, much like saving the children. There were no sequels, but Cobra Triangle is in the Rare Replay collection.

### The bottom line

Play it. Joe voted Essential: he couldn't say it isn't essential, and he liked how clean and textured it looks. Sean voted no. It should be experienced, but the less fun moments outweighed the fun ones, so it wasn't a complete package. Mike's vote is not clear from the recording.
