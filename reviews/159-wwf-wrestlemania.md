# 159 - WWF WrestleMania

Rare made this for Acclaim Entertainment in 1989: a wrestling game featuring Hulk Hogan and other WWF stars, with a tournament mode as its main feature and eight wrestlers to pick from. Sam joined Mike, Sean and Joe for this one. Sean put it plainly: not as jank as past wrestling games, but 100 percent a jank game.

### What works

- **Wrestlers who play differently.** They are not palette swaps. Only some can go off the top rope, Bam Bam Bigelow does his cartwheel, and only Hulk Hogan can slam Andre the Giant. Andre is slower but has much more reach.
- **Lore and music.** Hulk versus Macho Man feels like the real feud. Every wrestler has theme music, and Macho Man's is Pomp and Circumstance.
- **The pause sound.** It is the longest and most intricate we've had so far, a full song that sounds like something is starting. It keeps playing after you unpause.

### What doesn't

- **Sticky, half-missing controls.** Moves have a lead-up before they land. You can walk around the ring in 3D, but you can only attack facing your opponent on a flat plane, which feels like half the game is missing. Stuck in a corner, you take the hit to get out. Pinning uses different buttons per character, and the manual's pin instructions are wrong.
- **Nothing around the ring.** There is no crowd, only a black void, and no announcers or introductions. The managers are listed in the game and the manual but never appear. We recorded during the pandemic and noted the silence matches wrestling on TV right now.
- **Hard, and one mode.** Nobody on the recording beat the tournament. Hulk Hogan, saved for last, was the toughest, and playing as him is a huge advantage. We wanted a build-up to WrestleMania instead of single fights.

### Worth knowing

- **Power-ups.** Each wrestler gets a weird health power-up. Honky Tonk Man's looks like a guitar. Hulk Hogan's looks like a crucifix, a sword or an X, and we suggested an American flag or mustache wax.
- **Rare.** It is a step down after their hot start on the system, though not garbage. Unlike Slalom, there are no big butts.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List. Sam called it the best of the wrestling games we've played, but still lacking. Mike disagreed on the ranking: Pro Wrestling is the standard, and he pointed to Legends of WrestleMania on GameCube, PS2 and Xbox for the full presentation. Joe found more charm and attention to detail than the bad reviews he'd seen suggested, and would only call it a play-it for people into wrestling games anyway.
