# 286 - Heavy Barrel

Data East developed and published this top-down run and gun, and it may be the first game we've played that is about the weapon instead of the character. You collect six pieces of a giant gun, then get it for a short window. We enjoyed it, but nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List.

### What works

- **The weapons.** The Heavy Barrel doubles the size of your sprite, fires hilariously large shots and blows everything up quickly. The others hold up too: the flamethrower behaves like flames and hits the enemies behind the one it kills, and a grenade you poop forward sits there damaging bosses.
- **Controls that shouldn't work.** You can only fire in the direction you move, which made Commando feel janky. Here the view is top-down, so shooting and walking share an axis, and the game doesn't pin you in a corner.
- **Goofy deaths.** Sean liked setting dudes on fire and watching them swan dive onto their backs. The arcade version's death animation is funnier: airborne, three flips, dead.

### What doesn't

- **The big gun underdelivers.** It is only marginally wider than the flamethrower, with a faster fire rate. You can't choose when to use it. Grab the last piece and the 99 seconds start, even on an elevator with no enemies.
- **Same hallways.** The levels are hallways of different shapes and so many elevators. We never figured out why the grunts take the stairs.
- **Easy bosses.** They go down in a few hits. On the final boss, losing all your lives offered a continue right where you stood, twice, and then the credits rolled. Maybe continues are limited, but it felt like you couldn't die. It sounded like a Big Rigs bug.
- **Unfinished.** Textures pop out of the game when a boss battle starts, as if it was rushed.

### Worth knowing

- **The story.** A technician took the Heavy Barrel apart into six pieces and hid them. We blamed the manual: the mechanic is fine, the explanation feels reverse engineered, and nobody explained the treasure chests in Zelda.
- **Co-op.** It has real two-player co-op. Joe, our usual co-op player, skipped it, so we wondered who gets the Heavy Barrel when there is only one.
- **The arcade original.** Data East made that too, and it plays about the same, with an eight-way joystick and nicer graphics.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List, a unanimous "not essential" for a game we all had a good time with. It is tight but short and bare-bones. Sean compared it to a popcorn movie you forget you saw a year later.
