# 217 - Fester's Quest

Sunsoft made and published this in 1989, the first Addams Family video game, and it stars Uncle Fester against aliens who have kidnapped an entire city. It is a run-and-gun adventure with a top-down neighborhood and sewers, plus first-person 3D mazes. We liked how it looks and sounds. It is just no fun to play.

### What works

- **The presentation.** Cutscenes tell the story before you press start, and the sprite work looks advanced.
- **The music.** There aren't many tracks, but they jam. The overworld theme reminded us of the Laguna battle music in Final Fantasy VIII.
- **The bosses.** They look like they came from hell and never appeared on the TV show. You fight them on a black background like a shmup. One is a hunched-over chicken-worm with a gun that always points at you. They belong in Contra, not Fester's quest.

### What doesn't

- **The grind.** Fester starts so weak he is Feeblefester, "level negative seven." The first enemy takes about seven hits, and you hope enemies drop keys and light bulbs.
- **The gun.** Some guns fire wavy shots, and any wall stops a bullet, so in a hallway or sewer you can't hit what is in front of you. We read that the European release doesn't have this bug.
- **The 3D mazes.** Bare walls, nothing to do but navigate, and the screen blinks black and back to white between views, with no turning frame for your bearings.
- **The formula.** Get a key, unlock a house, get an item from a family member, take a sewer to the next area. We split on whether it feels open.
- **The family.** They show up as a full-screen pixel image saying who gave you what, with no dialogue. Wednesday's vice grips cure the half speed from bug stings and frog-people licks, and we couldn't work out why.

### Worth knowing

You get one life and infinite continues. Die at the UFO ship and you restart at the beginning, with the bosses gone and no way to refill your health and items. There are no passwords, so it is best played with save states.

We suspected it began as another game with the Addams Family on top, since the music doesn't feel like the show. The top-down parts do resemble Blaster Master, so Sunsoft may have reused its own engine.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List. We said fixing the wall bug, cutting the 3D mazes or removing the license wouldn't change that. It is not one of the worst NES games, but it is an oddity with really good music.
