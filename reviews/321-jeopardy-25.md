# 321 - Jeopardy! 25th Anniversary Edition

This is the third Jeopardy! on the NES for us, and we barely played it. Mike, Sean and Joe opened by celebrating six years of the podcast, then slid into what turned out to be a clip show built from our earlier Jeopardy! episodes, with categories like "Play the game" and "Get nostalgic" standing in for a review. We were unsure at first whether we were in one, and in the end it was the only honest way to cover a game that is still just Jeopardy!

### What the episode turned up

- **The same game as before.** Rare handled all the versions, and what we remembered was familiar: answers have to be exact, so "John Flame" for the Human Torch gets you nothing. Category names are hard to read, and a "Thick and thin" board could mean anything from flour to thin air. Choosing a contestant is about looks, and everyone comes out a strange pink, puke green or brown.
- **It does the job.** Joe went in expecting it to be atrociously boring. Alone, it isn't much fun, but with a friend it works surprisingly well, and there are enough questions to keep going for a while.
- **Nothing to fault, nothing to love.** We couldn't name anything the game gets wrong for the NES. We just aren't looking forward to playing the other two Rare versions again.

### Beyond the game

The clips brought back the first game ever removed from the Essential Games List, Excitebike, which came off after a vote on episode five that wasn't unanimous and was regretted right away. A trivia clue had us confirm that Rare is from the United Kingdom, the first third party on the NES not from Japan. One more Jeopardy! remains, Super Jeopardy!, which we plan to treat as a full clip show.

### The bottom line

Skip it. We all voted no, so it stays off the Essential Games List. Joe said it's still exactly what you'd expect from Jeopardy!, and we agreed that even a perfect Jeopardy! game would never be essential: it'd be like that sometimes. In 1988 it offered a lot to anyone who wanted it, but plenty of people find Jeopardy! a chore to watch, let alone play. If you want a trivia night with a friend, it works. Otherwise watch it weeknights at 7 on ABC.
