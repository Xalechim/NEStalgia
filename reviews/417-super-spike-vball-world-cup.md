# 417 - Super Spike V'Ball/Nintendo World Cup

Another cartridge episode, and we had already covered both games. Mike, Sean and Joe were here, and we treated it as a product with a SKU: the Sports Set cart that came with four controllers and an adapter. Most of the hour became a question the show has asked since 10-Yard Fight, which is what a sports game would need to be Essential.

### The bundle

- **Versus last week's set.** We all picked the Power Set with Mario and Duck Hunt, even counting the Power Pad you will probably throw out (or, as one joke had it, use as a towel).
- **Not the sports anyone wanted.** Volleyball is not a headline sport and soccer is not everyone's favorite. We would have wanted baseball, hockey or football, and been thrilled if the football turned out to be Tecmo Bowl.
- **Fine games, no lift.** We called Super Spike V'Ball and Nintendo World Cup fine, and putting them on one cart does not improve either.

### What a sports game needs to be essential

- **A short record.** A couple of sports games have made the list, Baseball Stars among them, so the bar is higher.
- **Nostalgia is a bad yardstick.** Many of our favorite sports games are ones we played young, for the rosters and the stars. Revisited, N64 hockey and Ken Griffey Jr. games are loved for the sounds, music and rosters, nowhere near the best ever.
- **What counts as a sport.** We settled on this: it has to exist in real life. That puts Madden, NFL Blitz (people on fire and all), NASCAR and motorsport in, and keeps Rocket League, Smash Brothers and Kung Fu kickball out.
- **Our picks.** Joe went for Tony Hawk's Pro Skater and SSX Tricky because they let you do what you never could. Sean named NBA Jam, NASCAR 98 (with the code that turned it into Twisted Metal) and Madden 08, where he put franchise teams in Mexico City and Calgary. We leaned toward arcade over sim, with Wii Sports, NFL Blitz and Backyard Baseball as examples, and liked the idea of an NBA Jam mode in every basketball game.
- **What turned us off.** Having to play Blades of Steel before Mario, a rally game that proved we cannot drive one, and NHL games that exposed us as phony coaches.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Two games on one cart do not make a better package, and neither is one we would send you to. We never voted: we agreed that offering them together does not put them on the Essential Games List, and joked about a Zelda and Zelda II combo pack. Whether this should be an episode at all got a shrug, and the answer was that it is a product with a SKU, so someone suggested re-recording the intro to say SKU.
