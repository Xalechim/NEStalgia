# 274 - Super Spike V'Ball

Technōs Japan made this for Nintendo in 1990, a home version of an arcade game. Mike, Sean and Joe were all there, fresh off Kings of the Beach. We liked this one better and called it a good volleyball game, but nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List.

### What works

- **Bump, set, spike.** One button touches the ball and another jumps, so you line up the jump and the swing yourself. Time it right and you get a big "kaboom." The skill ceiling is higher than the controls suggest.
- **The presentation.** 1960s Batman-style words like "wop" and "kaboom" pop up, a woman announces points and outs, and the screen scrolls with the ball, which adds motion to a hit.
- **The locations.** Daytona Beach, New York City, Chicago, Las Vegas and Los Angeles. Chicago has bears in the stands, New York just says "good food," and people show up to the beach in suits.
- **Billy and Jimmy.** The Double Dragon heroes are in it, so there are no athletes to learn.

### What doesn't

- **The clone.** You pick one character and your teammate is the same sprite, and the roster is too small for the choice to matter. We wanted a triangle system of offense, defense and technical types, where you can't have all three.
- **It plays itself at first.** Players do nothing until the ball is near, then target and volley on their own, so timing your spikes and mashing the button carries you early.
- **It's unforgiving.** There is no help if you almost hit the ball, and a whiff drops it in the sand with no flair, as if the whole world paused to watch you fail.
- **A mundane tournament.** Lose one and you're out. We daydreamed about a Gran Turismo-style career where winnings buy volleyballs and swimsuits, and mourned the missing coin toss.

### Worth knowing

- **Control swaps.** The game hands you whichever teammate is closer to the ball. It was jarring at first, but the ball moves too fast for a manual switch.
- **Cut content.** Wikipedia lists female teams as cut, but the data is still on the cart. A Game Genie code unlocks them, fully animated.
- **World Cup.** You face Japan, Italy, Australia, Mexico and Brazil, then the U.S. Navy and the USSR. We never reached the Navy. In the American version the Soviets are bald, pale, muscly men in Speedos, cut down a little from the Japanese one.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List. Kings of the Beach leaned on celebrity athletes, and this one needed none of that. Joe said it is good but will blend into the other volleyball games, and Sean wanted more goofball energy and wacky super moves, like dodgeball. A fun game, not essential.
