# 214 - The Black Bass

Another made this for Hot-B in 1989, and it is the first fishing game we have played on the NES. You are one of 200 anglers in a season-long tournament, starting at 200th place as a class C fisherman, and you climb by catching big black bass. There is little setup and a dry manual. The fight with the fish works. Most of what surrounds it is slow and short on feedback.

### What works

- **Reeling in a fish.** An endurance meter shows how tired the fish is, a sound cue ticks faster as the line nears breaking, and you steer around lily pads and weeds. It is the most legible part of the game, and in the first lake it was easy.

### What doesn't

- **Finding a spot.** You sail there, cast, then back out to the main menu to ask a guide whether bass are around. We couldn't see why that isn't on the boat screen.
- **Getting a bite.** It was trial and error with no feedback. Most of the time the fish turned around and left, whatever we tried. The manual's lure advice is thin: silver for a clear day is its example.
- **Fish that don't count.** Only black bass score. The manual says to give up on the others, yet the game still records them and congratulates you. We wanted boots and garbage, or a lure unlocked by a big pike.
- **Average weight.** Your rating follows the average weight of your bass, not the biggest, so one small fish drags you down.
- **Nothing changes.** The goal is the same in every lake, and moving up a class is just a new title on screen. We pitched weather per lake, a different fish per tournament, and an economy with a bass auction. Bass of the Wild.

### Worth knowing

Quit out and you get a very long password and a trip to the main menu, which is also the only way to check your record for the day. Have a notebook ready.

In the sequels section, the best pitch was Bassin's Black Bass on the SNES: a boat radar, a guide who looks like the main side character from Home Improvement, and graphics that look awesome in the fight.

### The bottom line

Skip it. It did not make the Essential Games List. Sean voted not essential: a fishing game needs to be interesting or relaxing, and this one is tedious, though the music is good. The other votes agreed. Once you land a bass you just go catch another, and it gets stale so fast. You can't have an essential game get stale.
