# 203 - The Adventures of Tom Sawyer

Winkysoft made this for SETA in 1989, a platformer where Tom dreams he has to rescue Becky across six big stages, and a second player gets Huckleberry Finn. We expected nothing and were surprised by how well it plays. What it adds up to is a pile of good ideas that never become a game.

### What works

- **It plays well.** It is no Super Mario Bros., but there is some post-jump input, and Joe found it better to control than some games we rate highly.
- **Enemy variety.** Every stage has different enemies, and late in the game they still start doing something new. The pirate dodges your rocks, which is cool and a little cheap.
- **The raft.** It is almost a shmup. You throw rocks from a raft, and your big jump doesn't sync with it, but you can correct in the air. It is used for exactly one level.

### What doesn't

- **The rocks.** The arc goes around enemies unless you hit the perfect spot, and enemies appear close with little warning.
- **Movement problems.** Stop short of the top of a ladder and you fall all the way back down. A fish jumps out of the water at the pixel you are standing on, unlike Mario's Cheep Cheaps.
- **Cheesed bosses.** Fire constantly at the octopus, the first boss, and it dies before its first attack.
- **Uneven difficulty.** You can avoid everything for stretches, then suddenly there are eight enemies, a balloon and thunderclouds shooting lightning.
- **No through line.** It is six different things, and none of them builds on the last. A cloud-riding bit lasts about 15 seconds when it could have been a whole level. Tom has no personality, just rocks. Give him a paintbrush, or let him bribe enemies.

### Worth knowing

The manual walks you through the whole game, including a text description of the final scene. It also explains that the gorilla is made of tiny monkeys and names him Sarudan, even though the game already shows the monkeys forming a cheer pyramid.

The only other Tom Sawyer game we know of is Square's Tom Sawyer, a Famicom-only RPG never translated into English, where one enemy can trigger the reset button on your controller.

### The bottom line

Skip it. We called it not essential. Sean said it should have been a different genre, maybe a Goonies-style adventure. Mike said you probably won't have a great time with it, and it doesn't reach hidden gem status. Joe found it fun and a fair pick for a random platformer you never played, but put it on his list of games that could have been really good, and weren't.
