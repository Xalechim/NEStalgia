# 171 - World Games

Rare made this for Milton Bradley in 1989: a pile of mini games where each sport comes from a different country, from weightlifting in Russia to sumo wrestling in Japan. Sam joined Mike, Sean and Joe for this one. We hated Winter Games, and this was a step up, but only to okay.

### What works

- **The world tour idea.** Each event opens on a splash screen about the sport and where it comes from. It is a good conceit.
- **Barrel jumping.** Three voters picked it as their favorite event, and a fourth picked the caber toss. You choose how many barrels to clear, which makes for a "hold my beer" moment. We topped out at 13 and figured 14 was possible. Hold down on the D-pad any time before you land and you stick it, which fixes the figure skating we hated in Winter Games.
- **Cliff diving.** It is free form, and the score depends on how close you stay to the cliff and when you pull out of your dive. The lower dives are harder and pay less, so there is no reason to do them.
- **Weightlifting.** Mike thought it translated best: up and down on the D-pad, timed to your lift, and hold too long and you drop it.

### What doesn't

- **No feedback.** You can't tell why you dropped the weight. Log rolling has two bars that look like alien glyphs and are not in the manual, and in bull riding nothing changes based on what buttons you hit.
- **Log rolling.** The computer refused to fall, one game ran about three minutes, and there is no sound during play until you splash in and the shark comes out.
- **Caber toss.** Joe learned from a video that you take a short running start, wait for the log to lean forward, then throw. Mike compared it to the javelin: you look like you're doing nothing, and you "might as well balance it on your head."
- **Too easy.** We kept getting gold.

### Worth knowing

- **Sumo.** Holding A means holding the opponent's belt, but trickier inputs lost and just pushing toward your opponent won.

### The bottom line

Skip it. Nobody voted it onto the Essential Games List. Barrel jumping won the event favorites, but as Sam put it, the game has "super janky controls" and "it's not super fun to play."
