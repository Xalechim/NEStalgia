Click or tap the TV to aim — the crosshair sticks where you put it. Try a duck, empty sky, or the lamp. Then pull the trigger.

Slow-mo stretches each frame to about a second so you can watch the check happen. On real hardware the whole thing takes two or three frames of a 60 fps picture — switch to real speed and it's just a flicker. (Hardware trivia: the gun reports “light seen” as a 0 on bit 3 of the controller port.)
