# NB 027 - Konami Wai Wai World

- Wai oh Wai did this game not come to the States?
- NES Longplay
- Super Smash Bros
  - EVERYONE IS HERE…do you know them?
- Plot
  - A mysterious alien being has infiltrated Konami World, and allied itself with the evil forces that already existed there.
  - Six of Konami's heroes have been defeated and imprisoned
  - Konami World's only remaining hope lies with its resident super hero: Konami Man and sidekick Konami Girl
  - Travel to various areas of Konami World and rescue the imprisoned heroes.
  - Then, once all of the heroes are reunited, the alien force must be
  - eradicated!
- Gameplay
  - Confusing start with the doors
  - Select a stage - but can’t really go to all of them
  - Characters
    - Konami Man
    - Konami Girl
      - No abilities
    - We know Simon, Mikey, Moai, and King Kong (just not the way we think we know King Kong)
    - Goemon and Fuuma are from Japanese only games
  - You collect bullets so quickly in the game, but you can’t use them without the gun
  - The exploration
    - Camera ONLY scrolls when you’re at the edge of the screen, really disappointing
    - Backtracking
    - Plenty to find, plenty to lose
    - The usefulness of the characters
  - Slots/Dice/Cards
    - Bonus games that can restore health, bring characters back to life, gain bullets
    - The Card Match game is not a true match game? Just a value match game lol
  - The characters
    - Swapping with Up+A
- Vic Viper and TwinBee show up as the SHMUP ships
  - There’s then a TwinBee and Gradius section. You’re free to swap between the ships by pressing select
- Boss Fights are not iconic characters (seemed like an easy fix)
  - Dracula is in the Castlevania level, but doesn’t look like him and isn’t the actual boss.
  - The ending boss fight is just against a 3 headed alien. Missed moment?
- Sequels and spinoffs
  - Wai Wai World 2 (should we play it?)
  - A mobile port of the game added Upa from Bio Miracle (a game we played for bytes) and Pentarou (from Antarctic Adventure) also from Bytes
