# NB 032 - Love Warrior Nicol

- Story Setup: The plot, detailed in the manual, involves Nicol and Stella’s teleportation device attracting the Demon Bull King, who kidnaps Stella. The in-game intro shows a Metroid-like alien abducting Stella during a romantic walk, with some humorous Engrish included.
- Gameplay Style: The gameplay resembles the on-foot sections of Blaster Master, with tighter controls and design. Each level, structured like a Zelda dungeon, involves methodical exploration, collecting three diamonds to power a hovercraft, and battling bosses.
- Action-Focused Levels: Unlike Zelda, Ai Senshi Nicol is all about action, with power-ups doubling as tools for unlocking new paths, similar to Metroid. Examples include a gun that destroys specific blocks and shoes that allow longer jumps.
- Underground Sections: Pits in each level lead to underground areas filled with hazards like lava or poison lakes. These sections serve as traps for mistimed jumps but also contain hidden items.
- Power-Up Hunting: Many optional power-ups are hidden in random blocks, encouraging players to shoot everything in search of upgrades.
- Control and Combat: The game features simple, responsive controls with eight-way movement and competent jumping mechanics. Combat is patient and strategic, with challenging bosses that require pattern recognition.
- Level Complexity: Early levels are straightforward, but later stages become more intricate, requiring repeated exploration and posing a challenge due to respawning enemies.
- Visuals and Technical Design: Ai Senshi Nicol boasts colorful, cartoony visuals with excellent sprite work, minimal flicker, and large bosses, making it look ahead of its time for a 1987 release.
- Music: The soundtrack, utilizing extra FDS sound channels, is catchy and fits the game’s light-hearted visuals, standing out among Konami’s best on the console.
- Legacy and Availability: Despite its quality, Ai Senshi Nicol was never released outside Japan. The game is fully in English and highly accessible, making it a lost classic worth trying.
