# NB 042 - Bubble Bath Babes

- 🕹️ General Overview
Released unlicensed in 1991 by Panesian, this game was technically a puzzle title in the tradition of Columns or Dr. Mario. It just happened to be distributed on black cartridges and definitely not sold at Toys "R" Us. But let’s talk about what really matters: bubbles, colors, combos, and matching tile mechanics.
- 👤 Story & Objective
- “This game doesn’t leave much to the imagination... about its tile-matching priorities.”
 You’re here for one reason: to pop some bubbles.
 Each round presents a grid where balls of various colors descend. The goal is to match three or more of the same color in a row—vertically, horizontally, or diagonally. Do that, and the screen clears a little. Don’t, and the screen clogs up faster than a dorm shower drain.
- Objective:
 Clear enough bubbles to move to the next round. Repeat.
 Nothing else going on here. Nothing to see. Just bubbles. Honest.
- 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics
- “You might think this is just a match-3 game, but once you get deep... into the mechanics, that is... it’s quite revealing.”
- Tile Matching:
 You're stacking colored spheres that fall in vertical trios. You can rotate the order and move the column left or right before dropping it. If you’ve played Columns on Genesis, this will feel suspiciously familiar.
- “Some people say this game is all about what’s on screen, but real fans know it’s all about what’s under the surface. Like chain reactions.”
- Chain Combos:
 Matching bubbles often leads to combo chains, especially if you clear bubbles that cause upper tiles to fall into new matching patterns. This is where the game gets unexpectedly satisfying—if you’re only focused on the gameplay, of course.
- “There’s definitely a learning curve. Things get harder as you progress. Again—we’re just talking level difficulty.”
- Difficulty Curve:
Starts manageable, but by level 4 or 5, the speed ramps up and so does the chaos. This is where the game becomes steamy. Ahem. In a bubble strategy sense.
- What makes a good falling-block puzzle game feel satisfying, and does Bubble Bath Babes deliver on that?
- Secret Technique – Level Select Cheat:
 Start a new game. When the STAGE message appears on screen, press Start to pause, then input:
 Up, Right, Down, Left.
 You can now use B to change the stage number and A to change the level number. Press Start twice to begin your custom challenge.
 A totally innocent cheat for those who simply want to test their bubble prowess under precise conditions.
- Level Clear screens:
- After you pop enough bubbles you’re greeted with a screen of a woman congratulating you on finishing the level. Gee thats awfully nice of her.
- 🎨 Graphics & Sound
- Visuals:
 Let’s talk about the real stars of the screen: the bubbles. They come in five vivid colors and have a glossy, almost metallic sheen to them. The animations are minimal, and the background never distracts you from your job: matching orbs.
- “The bubble physics are not bouncy at all”
- Audio:
 A surprisingly catchy loop plays as you work through levels. The sound design is simple, with satisfying “pop” sounds when bubbles vanish. That’s it. No commentary. Just sound and color harmony.
- 📀 Legacy & Reception
- Bubble Bath Babes is part of the infamous Panesian trilogy (alongside Peek-A-Boo Poker and Hot Slots), all unlicensed NES titles with the same engine.
- In the same year (1991), American Video Entertainment released a reskin under the name Mermaids of Atlantis.
- Meanwhile, Hacker International brought the original version to Japan as Soap Panic. Exact same gameplay. Same... everything. Just a different title. And still technically released in the U.S. by Panesian as well.
- And for those playing along at home: a Genesis / Mega Drive version exists too! Titled Magic Bubble (1993), it's a much more accessible, all-ages puzzle game that still uses the same falling-bubble mechanic, minus any distractions.
- Today, original cartridges are ultra-rare collector's items, often fetching ridiculous prices—You’d need a tub of money to put your hands on one.
