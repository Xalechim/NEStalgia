- How a Dodge Ball Card game works
  - Pick a team of 7 characters (4 Infielders and 3 Outfielders)
  - Each player has 3 stats: Attack, defense, stamina
  - You draw 7 cards and pick 4
  - There are four types of cards:
    - Regular
    - Pass
    - Line Cross
    - Super
  - Then it’s your card (as the thrower) vs the card of the target
    - A Regular card has three digits. One of the numbers will have the word "HIT" next to it which is important for when you are attacking. It will battle out vs another regular card's digit in the same row.
  - Each game is 10 minutes long and when this has elapsed the team with more players left will win.
