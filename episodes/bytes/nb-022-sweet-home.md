- Sweet Home is a role-playing video game developed and published by Capcom for the Family Computer in 1989. It is based on the Japanese horror film of the same name and tells the story of a team of five filmmakers exploring an old mansion in search of precious frescos hidden there.
  - It served as the main inspiration behind Resident Evil
    - When development began in 1993, Resident Evil was originally intended to be a remake of Sweet Home
    - Resident Evil was originally intended to have a first-person perspective influenced by Sweet Home's battles, before the perspective was changed during development.
    - Both games are set in a mansion with an intricate layout,[1] the story is told through the use of scattered notes, and there are multiple endings depending on how many characters survive.
    - They both have that door animation
