# 061 - Double Dribble

- Thick of September 1987 - 15 GAMES this month for the NES
- Konami is at it again tackling a different sport. Track and Field came out in April 87
- Arcade first in 86 so not as much of a gap.
- Crazy microtransaction:
- The arcade version was a timed play, where after every minute of play, the game was owed a credit if the score was tied or the computer is ahead. However, if the live player was ahead, a "free" minute was earned. Many average players could earn the first free minute, but this became increasingly more difficult to do as making 3-point shots became virtually impossible over the course of gameplay.
