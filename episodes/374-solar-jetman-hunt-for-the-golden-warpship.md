# 374 - Solar Jetman: Hunt for the Golden Warpship

- 🎮 Title/Overview:
 Solar Jetman: Hunt for the Golden Warpship is the third game in the Jetman series, following Jetpac (1983) and Lunar Jetman (1984). It was released for the NES in 1990 (NA) and 1991 (EU), and later re-released on Nintendo Switch Online.
- 🕹️ Core Objective:
- Navigate Jetman’s pod through caverns across 12 alien worlds to retrieve pieces of the Golden Warpship.
- Once all parts are assembled, players pilot the completed Warpship to the final showdown.
- 🚀 Pod Physics and Gravity:
- Movement is influenced by gravity, inertia, and momentum.
- Players must control thrust carefully to avoid crashing into terrain or enemies.
- Docking with the mothership to refuel and repair requires precision.
- 🛢️ Fuel and Resource Management:
- Fuel depletes gradually with thrust use, requiring frequent refills.
- Resources such as gems and fuel cells can be collected and sold for upgrades.
- 💥 Combat and Defense:
- Pods are equipped with basic weapons but can be upgraded.
- Shields offer protection but consume energy, creating a balance between offense and defense.
- ⚙️ Upgrades and Shop System:
- Earned resources can be spent on better engines, stronger shields, and specialized weapons.
- Upgrades improve maneuverability, fuel efficiency, and firepower.
- 🌌 Risk and Reward Balance:
- Later levels introduce more challenging gravitational forces and hostile enemies.
- Exploring distant areas yields high-value resources but increases risk.
- 🕹️ Development and Design:
 Developed by Zippo Games and Rare, the game was influenced by Thrust (1986). It was planned for Amiga, Commodore 64, and Atari ST but these versions were canceled.
- 🏆 Reception and Legacy:
 The game received praise for its graphics and innovative gameplay but was criticized for its unforgiving mechanics. It gained a cult following over time and remains a notable entry in Rare’s history.
