# 411 - Ninja Crusaders

- They struck without warning!
- 1. General Overview – NES Version
- Elevator pitch
Side-scrolling action platformer where two ninjas, Talon and Blade, take on an alien invasion… with the ability to transform into animals mid-level. Developed by NMK and published by American Sammy on NES in 1990.
- Animorphs, Ranma ½ (Cold water triggers the cursed form. Hot water reverses you back to your original form.), The Emperor’s New Groove, Wonder Boy III: The Dragon’s Trap (You start as a human hero, but early on you’re cursed into an animal-man form.)
- NINJA GAIDEN meets CONTRA at least in plot
- COWBOYS VS ALIENS MOVIE with Daniel Craig and Harrison Ford
- Core identity
 Fast, fairly short stages… one-hit deaths… and four weapons that double as animal forms (tiger, sea scorpion, falcon, dragon). The game sits in that Ninja Gaiden / Shadow of the Ninja lane, but its animal system is its hook.
- Structure
 Roughly ten stages, each only a minute or two if you know them, but packed with enemies and jumps that force memorization.
- Reputation
  - Often called an overlooked ninja game… solid but overshadowed by Ninja Gaiden and Contra. Questicle+2retrovania-vgjunk.blogspot.com+2
  - Has a small cult following that loves the “B-movie” premise of ninjas vs aliens and the transformations, while also calling out some cheap enemy spawns. Reddit+2retrovania-vgjunk.blogspot.com+2
- Offbeat questions for this section
- Does the premise “ninja vs aliens” feel intentionally like a B-movie, or like a sincere late-80s attempt to mash two cool things together?
- 2. Story & Objective
- Manual / back-of-box premise
 Alien invaders wipe out Earth’s defenses in one surprise strike. Cities are destroyed, the army is useless, and a small resistance of ninja is all that’s left. The ancient masters decide to send two elite ninjas, Talon and Blade, to destroy the alien force “Inimicus” and reclaim Japan.
- In-game storytelling
  - All of this is essentially manual text; in the actual game, you get almost no story until the ending.
  - Stages scroll from left to right, bosses appear, you beat them, and move on.
  - Only at the end do you get a brief text wrap-up about defeating the invaders. vgmuseum.com+1
- Tone
 The tone is mostly implied: alien bases, bio-mechanical bosses, ruined cities.
- Offbeat questions for this section
- If you didn’t read the manual, would you know enough from the game to care beyond gameplay mechanics. Is it cool enough to want to keep going sans story?

- Progression and continues

- 4. Weapons & Animal Transformations
- This is the thing that makes Ninja Crusaders actually interesting to talk about.
- How transformations work
  - You hold the B button to morph into your animal, and hold again to change back. GameFAQs+2Video Game Sage+2
  - Each form radically changes how you navigate the level… sometimes they’re a shortcut, sometimes a trap.
- Four main weapons, four animal forms Wikipedia+2GameFAQs+2
  - Shuriken → Tiger
    - Default weapon. Longest range but weakest damage.
    - Transformation: fast tiger form… low to the ground, high jump, quick claw swipe, but short reach.
  - Kusarigama (chain-sickle) → Sea Scorpion
    - Shorter range, more power, attack has an arc.
    - Transformation: sea scorpion that swims well and can fight underwater, but moves slowly on land.
  - Bō staff → Falcon
    - Medium range, good power, nice arc for hitting things above/below.
    - Transformation: falcon that can fly over everything but cannot attack, turning stages into navigation puzzles.
  - Sword → Dragon
    - Rare weapon with strong close-range slash.
    - Transformation: screen-clearing dragon… you fly, you’re invincible, you kill everything in one touch, but only for a few seconds and effectively only once per sword pickup.
- Late-game weapon drought
 At least one detailed blog notes that in the latter half of the game you basically stop seeing staff and sword, forcing you to lean on shuriken or kusarigama and making the transformation mechanic feel more limited just as the game gets hardest. retrovania-vgjunk.blogspot.com+1
- Offbeat questions for this section
- Which weapon/animal pairing actually feels “correct” for most of the game… and which ones feel like cool ideas that real players rarely risk using?
- Does the scarcity of staff and sword in later stages make the dragon/falcon forms feel special… or just underused?
- If you were remaking Ninja Crusaders, would you keep transformations tied to weapons, or separate them so you could freely pick your form?
- Maybe most importantly, what is the message from the devs about this return to the animal kingdom to help you stop aliens from taking Earth?
- 5. Enemies, Bosses & Alien Weirdness
- Enemy types
  - Humanoid soldiers with guns and melee weapons.
  - Flying drones, mechs, and organic aliens that feel a bit Contra-ish.
  - Many enemies move very quickly or spawn in from the edges of the screen with little warning
- Boss design
 Bosses include:
  - Alien guardians, skeletal tanks, giant bio-monsters, and a final multi-phase alien overlord. YouTube+2YouTube+2
  - Patterns are learnable, but early attempts can feel brutal thanks to one-hit deaths and sparse platforming around them.
- Offbeat questions for this section
- Do the enemies feel like a cohesive alien army, or a grab bag of “whatever looked cool to design that week”?
- Are the bosses memorable on their own terms, or mostly as obstacles in an otherwise straight sprint through the game?
- 7. Co-op, Lives Tricks & “Only If You Played” Stuff
- Two-player simultaneous play
  - You can play as Talon and Blade together through the whole campaign. Wikipedia+2gamesdb.launchbox-app.com+2
  - In 2P, when one player dies, the other keeps going and the dead player respawns instead of the whole level resetting… which massively lowers the effective difficulty if you coordinate.
- Co-op strategy
 A well-known forum tip: hoard lives for both players, then on the final boss make the player with more reserves play aggressively while the other hangs back… you churn through lives but keep sustained damage on the boss thanks to instant respawns. Video Game Sage
- Speed-clear potential
 Experienced players can clear the whole game in under 20 minutes by abusing transformations, knowing spawn timings, and minimizing deaths. YouTube+1
- Offbeat questions for this section
- Would you consider the 2-player respawn behavior a clever “intended” strategy, or basically an exploit that makes the game too soft?
- 9. Context, Comparisons & Legacy
- Part of the 1990 ninja wave
  - Released in the same general window as Ninja Gaiden II, Little Ninja Brothers, and even yet to be reviewed Shadow of the Ninja (both 1990 NES ninja action games).
- No direct sequels
  - NMK moved onto Arcade Shooters like Thunder Dragon, Black Heart, and Hacha Mecha Fighter which are all available as part of Arcade Archives.
