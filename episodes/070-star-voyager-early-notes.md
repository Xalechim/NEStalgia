# 070 - Star Voyager

- September 1987
- Developed by ASCII Entertainment. Only 6 games from them on the NES. They dissolved in 2010 long after their exit from Video Games.
- Published by Acclaim who previously brought us 3D World Runners which while being a few weeks back from us is still the same month as Star Voyager
- Originally called Cosmo Genesis
- Controls - https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Star_Voyager_(NES)
- The player's primary goal is to eliminate all enemy fleets before they can surround the stationary CosmoStation Noah.
- Can also visit 8 planets, 5 other space stations, Asteroid Field, Black Hole
- 10-Yard Fight footsteps as you get into cockpit
