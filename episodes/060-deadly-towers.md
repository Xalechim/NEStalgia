# 060 - Deadly Towers

- ARPG
- Co-Developed by Lenar and Tamtex (owned by Irem) for Irem
- Broderbund published in NA (best known for Apple II games and Prince of Persia)
- Lenar involved with video game development, musical instrument production, mechatronics, and CG production for TV shows.
- Deadly Towers was called Evil Bell on Famicom. The Japan Cart had a red LED which lit up when turned on.
- The game was almost called HELLS BELLS in NA, but NOA refused.
- LONGEST PLOT EVER - it keeps going and going
- Basically in a nutshell - Rubas, the Devil of Darkness, is taking over the kingdom with the power of the seven magic bells.
- Non-Linear Gameplay - Does it work?
- At the top of each tower is a boss
- SO MANY SCREENS - The Tenth maze has 235 screens
- ON DIFFICULTY
- Weiss stated: "We did a lot of testing of the game and we didn't actually find it that difficult at Brøderbund, and I think we wanted something more challenging than previously published titles to round out our portfolio.”
