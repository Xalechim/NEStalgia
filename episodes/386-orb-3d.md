# 386 - Orb-3D

- The Fate of the Universe is in your Hands
- 🕹️ General Overview
- Released in 1990 for NES by Hi-Tech Expressions.
- Originally developed as a 3D PC game by Software Toolworks, known for The Chessmaster and other educational tool/games
- You control a bouncing energy orb in first-person, ricocheting it through space mazes and puzzles.
- One of the earliest NES games to simulate 3D space… kinda.
- Notably includes a 3D depth illusion and cursor-based paddle control.
- 👾 Story & Objective
- You're a space pilot tasked with destroying the evil wizard Krohn, who rules the galaxy from the fortress ORBS-A-ZERK.
- To do so, you must conquer 30+ "spheres," each a puzzle-filled room floating in space.
- The ultimate goal is to defeat Krohn and restore peace to the galaxy…
- 🌀 Gameplay Mechanics
- Third person POV of the orb and the targets with the paddle on the screen's edges
- You bounce the orb into targets on the screen—sort of like 3D Breakout meets pinball.
- Use the D-pad to curve, angle, and deflect the orb, adjusting depth and trajectory.
- Each room has different puzzle elements: targets, switches, warp holes, energy drains.
- You have limited energy—if it runs out, it’s game over.

- 🎨 Graphics & Sound
- Uses forced perspective and shading to create a pseudo-3D look on NES hardware.
- Minimal but functional UI, emphasizing clarity over flair.
- Music and sound effects are sparse, with bleeps and sci-fi stings.
