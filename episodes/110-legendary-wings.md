# 110 - Legendary Wings

- IF YOU FAIL, MANKIND IS LOST. TAKE WING, LEGENDARY SOLDIERS
- Introductions
- Overview
  - Capcom is back! Last seen with Gun.Smoke (and Mega Man before that)
  - Fantasy-themed shoot-'em-up both Horizontal and Vertical.
  - Set in a distant future where an alien supercomputer named "Dark", which has been helping human civilization achieve a new state of enlightenment. He has suddenly rebelled against mankind. Two young warriors are given the Wings of Love and Courage by the God of War Ares in order to destroy Dark and ensure mankind's survival.
- Gameplay
  - SHMUP
  - Power-Ups
    - The player can improve their character's firepower to four levels: starting with the normal gun, the player can improve it to a twin laser, a penetration beam and a three-way flame shot.
  - Damage
    - Your power ups are your health
- Spinoffs
  - Michelle Heart makes an appearance in the fighting game Marvel vs. Capcom: Clash of Super Heroes as one of several support characters who assist the main fighters in combat.
  - In the tactical role-playing game Namco x Capcom, the character of Sylphie (the shopkeeper from Forgotten Worlds) dresses up as Michelle Heart when she performs one of her special attacks.
  - Michelle Heart is also featured in Project X Zone 2 as a cameo character in Captain Commando's Solo Unit attack.
