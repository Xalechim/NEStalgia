# 260 - Kings of the Beach

- Dig it!?
- NES Longplay
- Introductions
- Overview
  - Play as Sinjin Smith and Randy Stoklos. The game features three modes of play: practice, match play and tournament.
- Gameplay
  - Tournament
    - progress through five beaches (San Diego, Chicago, Waikiki, Rio de Janeiro, and Australia) filled with increasingly challenging opponents as you attempt to win it all.
  - Characters
    - Smith and Stoklos are joined by Ron Von Hagen, Tim Hovland and Mike Dodd as the only 'real' volleyball players featured in the game
    - EA spiced up the competition with some notorious characters from other games, including Hard Hat Mack and Lester from Skate or Die and Ski or Die.
  - the ability to 'argue' calls with the referee, which occasionally allows players to get a point overturned. However, if players argue too much, the referee may penalize them with a red card and deduct a point.
- The original game box had a press-on body tattoo inside.
- Sequels and Spinoffs
  - EA would never make another Volleyball Game.
