# 220 - Nfl

- Lead your team all the way to the Super Bowl
- NES Longplay
- Introductions
  - Football is back already! Well no we’re in a bit of an offseason in real life, but let’s check in on those Bills
- Overview
  - This is another NES football game, but this time with the NFL license.
  - They are bragging about being able to say SUPER BOWL
  - They put it in bold on the back of the box that this is the only licensed NFL NES game
  - And obviously that’s gonna make it sell well
  - AND YET, NO NFLPA
- Gameplay
  - The appeal of an NFL game is having NFL teams, but they didn’t make the teams unique instead you choose from 6 potentially balanced types of teams.
    - On the one hand, this is great if your team sucked that year, on the other hand it makes it feel more generic than an NFL game
  - They do try to customize the 6 team types with handicaps as well
  - The offensive playbook
    - Seperate from the manual
    - A unique way to create a lot of plays and take away the ability to screen loo
  - So let’s not breakdown football for everyone again, instead let’s talk about the flow of the game. Football, to me, as a low informed watcher, is mostly a game that is very segmented between plays and playbooks.
    - 3 stages - The scoreboard, the playbook, the 40 yard screen
      - Speaking of the 40 yard screen what do you think about the then zoom in to the 15 yard screen. It feels empty?
  - The slow mo during the play, effective or glitchy?
  - The computer is practice mode. The real battle is with friends
- Sequels and Spinoffs
  - This game was the first since NFL Football - released in 1979 for the Intellivision - to get an official National Football League license.
