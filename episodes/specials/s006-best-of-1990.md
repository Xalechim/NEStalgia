- AWARDS
- The “Atari Crash” Award (Most Chaotic Design Decision)
- Nominees
- Silver Surfer — one-hit deaths in a crowded shmup lane
- Dragon Warrior II — encounter spikes + late-game gauntlet
- Ninja Gaiden II — wind physics + bird RNG
- The Punisher — on-rail shooter, no zapper
- Werewolf: The Last Warrior — backflip invincibilities
- The “World 1-1” Award (Best Tutorial Without a Tutorial)
- Nominees
- Super Mario Bros. 3 — 1-1 teach everything again?
- Chip ’n Dale: Rescue Rangers — Stage 1
- TMNT II: The Arcade Game — first street teaches spacing/throws
- The “Manual Was The Game” Award (Best Packaging)
- Nominees
- StarTropics — the letter you had to dip in water
- Final Fantasy — the manual that’s basically a strategy guide
- Dragon Warrior II — maps/charts that save your sanity
- Ultima IV — 64 Page Full Color Guide complete with history of Ultima so far
- The “Italian Plumber on Mushrooms” Award (Best Playable Character)
- Nominees
- Mega Man (Mega Man 3) — slide + usual weapon rotation
- Trevor Belmont (Castlevania III) — swap-able buddies, more flexible kit
- Mike Jones (StarTropics) — yo-yo hero
- Jackie Chan — tight responsive kung fu action
