# 399 - The Silver Surfer

- You are all that can stop the universe from entering an endless age of darkness and distress
- Introduction
- This is the dude from the Capri Sun commercials yeah?
- Joe Satriani’s classic guitar album “Surfing with the Alien” used Silver Surfer cover art for decades, but the image was removed in 2018 when the Marvel license lapsed.
- Origins
- Created by Jack Kirby and Stan Lee. First appearance: Fantastic Four #48 (1966) “The Galactus Trilogy.”
- Real name: Norrin Radd of Zenn-La. To save his world, he volunteers to serve Galactus and is remade as the Silver Surfer, wielder of the Power Cosmic.
- From herald to hero (and occasional antagonist)
- Rebels against Galactus to protect Earth. As punishment, Galactus strands him within Earth’s barrier.
- Notable storylines and arcs
- Silver Surfer (1968–1970) by Lee and John Buscema: grand, moral fables and the definitive tragic tone.
- Ongoing in the late 80s and 90s (Englehart, Starlin, Marz): frees himself from Earth, deep dives into Kree vs. Skrull, Adam Warlock, Thanos, Infinity Gauntlet.
- “The Herald Ordeal” and other 90s arcs: examines what it means to be a herald, with clashes among Galactus’s servants.
- Annihilation (2006): cosmic war. He briefly resumes herald duties to counter existential threats.
- What it is: Software Creations’ 1990 Marvel‑licensed scrolling shooter for NES, published by Arcadia Systems. Alternates between side‑scrolling and overhead stages with strict one‑hit deaths.
- Why it matters: Legendary for its difficulty and for Tim & Geoff Follin’s blistering soundtrack—often cited among the NES’s most impressive audio showcases.
- Release, Credits, and Context
- Release: November 1990 (NES, North America). Single‑player; alternating 2‑player option from the title screen.
- Credits (manual): Directed by Graeme Devine & Rob Landeros; programming Kevin Edwards; art Neal Sutton, Craig Houston, Anthony Anderson; music Tim & Geoff Follin.
- Comics tie‑in: Story framing uses Galactus sending the Surfer to stop pieces of a portal/cosmic device falling into the Magik Domain.
  - Covered in Awesome Comic story in the manual
- Controls & Core Loop (teach early)
- A: Fire (rapid). Select: Smart Bomb (screen‑clear). B: Adjust orb position (after you collect an orb/“option”).
- Goal: Pick a villain to face off against. Survive three sections of their world; defeat a miniboss at the end of sections 1–2; face a Marvel villain at section 3; assemble device pieces; unlock the final Magik Domain.
- One‑hit deaths: Any projectile or solid scenery (walls, spikes, statues, architecture) kills you and strips all power‑ups.

- Power‑Ups & Systems (be specific)
- The surfer feels weak
- Turbo mode is the real mode
- It takes so long to get the orbs, but they are so worth it
- Enemies take multiple hits.
- F = Cosmic Power (shot strength/shape): 5 tiers. Starts as twin pellets; at higher tiers adds bullets and double hit‑power (your orb shots scale too). After the 5th F, extra F’s yield bonus points.
- B = Smart Bombs: Stocks up to 5; press Select to nuke non‑boss enemies/bullets.
- S (red) = Speed‑Up: One pick grants max acceleration; extra red S’s don’t stack.
- S (silver) = 1UP: Extra life.
- Orbs (“options”): Add a secondary shot; one orb usable in horizontal sections; up to two in vertical sections.
  - Horizontal orb cycle (B): Front → Below → Behind → Front.
  - Vertical orb cycle (B): Side → Behind → Front → Side.
- Lives/continues: Start with 5 lives (1 in play + 4 extra). Death resets you to a section checkpoint with base power.
- Presentation & Sound
- Visuals: Clean spritework; readable bullets; sometimes muddy background/foreground separation causes “wall bonks.”
- Audio: Follin brothers deliver multi‑layered, arpeggio‑dense track, but not too many of them
- Difficulty & Mastery
- Hitbox reality: The board counts—treat the Surfer’s tip and tail as live. Test safe lanes before committing.
- Orb discipline: In side‑scroll, set Below for ground clutter; in overhead, pre‑set Side before entering spawn corridors.
- Bomb economy: Spend bombs to keep power—a saved bomb isn’t worth losing F×4 + orbs.
- Speed control: One red S is enough for most; pass extras if control suffers.
- Practice loop: Use stage select to learn specific problem rooms; remember you restart a section on death.
- Stage Select & Suggested Order (use at the first map)
- Open wheel lets you tackle five worlds in any order: Reptyl, Mephisto, Possessor, Firelord, Skrull Emperor (Kylor).
- World‑by‑World Notes (ask questions where they happen)
- Stages are long in 3 sections per stage
- Very little Marvel inside the stages. Odd enemies. Mephisto has flyings skulls and eyeballs and ghosts sure, but then there’s like 2 14th century cannons
- Recommended ramp (from the manual): Start with Reptyl or Mephisto; save Firelord for later.

- Reptyl’s Domain
- Look/flow: Organic caverns and rocky corridors; lots of narrow tunnels and floor/ceiling scrapes that punish the hitbox.
- Threat mix: Bullet spam from stationary turrets; erratic orb swarms; tight squeezes that demand Below‑orb coverage in side‑scroll and Side‑orb in overhead.
- Boss: Reptyl with add‑ons; prioritize safespots and bomb if your orbs are poorly aligned.
- Mephisto’s Realm
- Look/flow: Gothic “hell” motifs—doors with spawning ghosts, gargoyles, bats, and hazard props that look passable but aren’t.
- Threat mix: Candle projectiles that drift; eyeball volleys; miniboss heads that demand shot cancels. Great early spot to learn Front → Below orb swaps.
- Boss: Mephisto patterns reward staying mid‑screen and timing bombs for wave overlaps.
- Possessor’s World
- Look/flow: Tech corridors, raising/lowering columns; many overhead sections with pincer spawns.
- Threat mix: Ceiling/floor turrets that trap you in the center; green droids; orb geysers from pipes.
- Boss: Possessor—safer to keep Front + Side orbs and move minimally.
- Firelord’s System
- Look/flow: Volcanoes, falling embers, collapsing supports; many places where the floor “looks” background but isn’t.
- Threat mix: Lava droplets + bats in overlapping rhythms; heavy need for bomb discipline.
- Boss: Firelord—projectile curtains plus environmental collision; enter with bombs and at least F×3.
- Skrull Emperor (Kylor)
- Look/flow: Cold metallic bases; fast turret locks; late‑game speed checks.
- Threat mix: Overhead gauntlets where Side → Behind orb swaps clear flanks; horizontal sections reward patient bottom‑third routing.
- Boss: Kylor (Skrull Emperor) with escorts; watch for last‑second spawns near walls.
- Final World – Magik Domain
- Unlock: Clear the five worlds to access this final gauntlet.
- Feel: Mixed geometry from prior worlds with denser, faster spawns; the ambiguous final boss fuels years of debate. Who is this purple guy and why does the portrait look like Mr. Sinister?
