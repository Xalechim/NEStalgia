# 077 - Rad Racer

- Introductions - Espo
  - Originally known as Highway Star in Japan
- Development History - Espo
  - Square is back at it again with more 3D sim
    - Square owner Masafumi Miyamoto wanted to demonstrate Gebelli's 3D programming techniques.
  - In Japan, it is one of the few titles for the system designed for use with Nintendo's Famicom 3D System peripheral for 3D experience.
- First Impressions - Everyone
  - Rad Racer is only the second “racing” game we have had. And I like the style! (Espo)
- Gameplay
  - Players drive a Ferrari 328 or a Formula One racing car through a race course.
  - Rally through a course and its checkpoints before the timer expires
  - Even if time runs out, the vehicle can continue to coast for a while; if the vehicle reaches a checkpoint before running out of momentum, the game continues.
- Additional Thoughts
  - OutRun exists, should this game matter? (Yes, because it’s on the NES)
  - Rad Racer II
    - NES North America only. Considered inferior by players
  - These are the Square Games that will lead us to the creation of Final Fantasy
